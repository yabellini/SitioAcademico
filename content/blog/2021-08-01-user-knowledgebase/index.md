The useR! knowledgebase’s goal is to build and maintain resources for organizing the useR! conferences. It was started as part of the Google Season of Docs project in 2021 (Proposal) and gathers information compiled by former useR! organizing teams and members of Forwards, the R Foundation taskforce on women and other under-represented groups.
