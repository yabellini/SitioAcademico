The Bio Network for Training, BioNT consortium with all its international partners will host the second European CarpentryConnect event from November 12 to 14, 2024. This event will be happening alongside the BioNT community event, creating a great opportunity for collaboration and networking. It will be held at EMBL - Heidelberg in Germany

Yanina Bellini Saibene is presenting a keynote at the event around measuring outcomes, results, activities, and impact of communities of practice.
