rOpenSci’s activities and spaces are supported by a Code of Conduct that applies to all people participating in the rOpenSci community, including rOpenSci staff and leadership. It applies to all modes of interaction including GitHub project repositories, the rOpenSci discussion forum, Slack, Community Calls, Co-working and social sessions, and in person at rOpenSci-hosted events, including affiliated social gatherings. Our Code of Conduct is developed and enforced by a committee including rOpenSci staff and an independent community member.
Here we report our annual review of rOpenSci’s Code of Conduct, reporting process, and internal guidelines for handling reports and enforcement.
