I was chair of the 49 Jaiio. Since 1961, the JAIIOs, Argentine Computer Conference, organized by the Sociedad Argentina de Informática (SADIO), have been held, where papers published in Annals are presented in parallel sessions, research results and experiences in the field of Computer Science are discussed on different topics, also developing conferences and meetings with the assistance of Argentine and foreign researchers, students and professionals.

The JAIIOs are organized as a set of separate symposia, each dedicated to a specific topic, lasting one or two days, in such a way as to allow the interaction of their participants.
