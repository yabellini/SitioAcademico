Although it is not possible to meet in person, the magic of R-Ladies does not stop and makes virtual encounters possible. In this meetup we will tell you some tips and tools so that any interactive virtual event (for example, class, workshop or online panel) is a success without additional stress when doing it. If you organize R-Ladies meetups, you will find where and how to access the infrastructure that will allow you to organize online meetups for your chapter. If you do not organize R-Ladies meetups, you will also find useful material for all kinds of online events that you organize or participate in.

These contents are brought to us by Latin American R-Ladies, mostly certified to teach by RStudio Education and The Carpentries. Most are also part of MetaDocencia, an organization that since the end of March has held more than 25 interactive workshops teaching online teaching to more than 300 Spanish-speaking teachers with excellent results. They all have vast experience telecommuting and teaching online.
