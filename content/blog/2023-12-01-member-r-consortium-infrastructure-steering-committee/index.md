I'm part of the RConsortium Infrastructure Steering Committee (ISC). This committee is responsible for the identification, selection and oversight of infrastructure projects, as well as directing best practices and community leadership within the R community. Voting representatives are appointed by the Platinum and Silver members of the R Consortium, as well as an elected member from the Silver membership class, and the project leads for all top-level projects as directed in the ISC Charter.

I have served on this committee during 2022 and 2023.  I'm still holding this position.
