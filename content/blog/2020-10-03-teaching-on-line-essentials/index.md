We are going to introduce basic good practices for managing the online classroom. Every detail of the workshop, starting with the pre-registration form until the return request after the workshop is finished, passing through the execution of the workshop, are a demonstration of strategies that can be adopted or adapted to any teaching task that happens interacting simultaneously with students. During the workshop we will have a demonstration of how to contact and prepare students to join the synchronous class, how to manage a virtual classroom of 20 people using Zoom, we will see examples to create a positive environment and we will have an introduction to pedagogical concepts of focused class design in the audience, schematization of content with concept maps, formative evaluation with diagnostic power, participation in a collaborative document to take notes and solve formative evaluations and other pedagogical tools based on evidence and easy to implement.
