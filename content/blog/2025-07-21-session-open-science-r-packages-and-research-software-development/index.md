The 2025 CSID Network Annual General Meeting will take place in Thailand from July 20 to July 26, 2025. This event is an opportunity for members of the CSID Network to come together, share knowledge, and discuss the future of the network.
Yani Bellini Saibene will attend the event in person and will host a session on rOpenSci activities, tools and resources around research open source software, R package development and software peer review.
