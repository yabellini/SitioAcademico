Last week I got the email from GitHub that renew my status as a GitHub Star again!. The text of the email said:


> Congratulations yabellini!
> 
> We’re thrilled to let you know that you have been chosen to be a **GitHub Star for 2025**. Thank you for all your continued work and dedication to supporting and influencing the entire GitHub community over the past 12 months!

> You are one of around **70 amazing GitHub Stars** selected from the community of over **150+ million developers on GitHub!** From creating content to giving us feedback to educating and sharing with your communities as they grow, you've played a tremendous role in helping users keep up with the rapid pace of change. You’ve been a shining light in what has continued to be a difficult year for many. Thank you for your passion to inspire, educate, and grow the community. ❤️


I am very grateful to the people in the R community who originally nominated me and for all the communities that I am part of that allow me to do this work of bringing coding and open science to more people around the world: [rOpenSci](https://ropensci.org), [LatinR](https://latinr.org), [R-Ladies](https://rladies.org) and [The Carpentries](https://carpentries.org). 

You can check my GitHub Star profile in the program webpage here: https://stars.github.com/profiles/yabellini/
