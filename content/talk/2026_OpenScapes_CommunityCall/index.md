AI is changing why people come to our communities, how they find us, and how they interact with each other. In this Openscapes Community Call, Yanina shares three stories — from LatinR's beginnings on a Slack message, to the rOpenSci Champions Program, to rOpenSci's software peer review in the era of AI — and draws an idea for community managers and an idea for community members from each one. AI gives you answers. Communities give you people, and the judgment and trust that come with them.
