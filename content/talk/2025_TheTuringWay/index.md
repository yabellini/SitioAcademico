The Turing Way and Software Sustainability Institute have teamed up to design and host a series of conversations on "governance" with researchers, contributors and leaders in open science. This event is for everyone who wants to engage with the decision-making at any or all levels and make those processes transparent, collaborative, and sustainable.

Learning from Each Other's Journeys: Case Studies from Open Initiatives - featuring Aleksandra Nenadic (SSI), Yanina Bellini Saibene (rOpenSci and R-Ladies), Chris Holdgraf (2i2c and Jupyter) and Yo Yehudi (OLS)
